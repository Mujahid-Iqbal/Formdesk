One pilot.
Ten relevant conversations.

A working offer and a prioritized starting list for independent desk makers.

Website evidence and public contact routes were researched. Fit scores and sales angles are hypotheses. No buyer interest, budget, decision-maker identity, or absence of an existing 3D tool is verified. Nobody has been contacted.

The offer: $500 USD

One simple rectangular desk, four surface finishes, two frame colours, two simple accessories, approved width/depth limits, one pricing formula/currency, branding, and quote delivery to one inbox.

Payment: $250 to start + $250 after acceptance. Target: 10 working days after approved inputs and access. One revision round, source handover, and 14 days of defect fixes.

Optional care: $39/month, up to 30 minutes of updates. Hosting, domain, provider fees and applicable tax agreed separately. No guaranteed sales result.

Full scope, inputs, exclusions and acceptance checks are in the pilot brief.

How to use this list

  1. Start with the first five. Recheck the live product and current ordering availability, then use the listed public business route.
  2. Use the tailored opening to ask a process question. The objective is a 15-minute discovery conversation, not an immediate contract.
  3. Ask: how many custom desk enquiries do you receive, which details are usually missing, and do you have a repeatable model with approved pricing rules?
  4. Only offer the pilot if one simple model fits. Ask for the product specification before confirming the fixed scope and delivery date.
  5. Record the response and next step. If appropriate, send one relevant follow-up after 5–7 working days; respect a decline or request to stop.

Launch dependencies: the hosted Site remains owner-private, so prospects cannot yet freely open the links. Share access deliberately before outreach. A business contact email has not been supplied; the sales page downloads the offer and transparently says it does not submit enquiries. Drafts contain a signature placeholder.
